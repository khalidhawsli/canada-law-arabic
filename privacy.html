What we collect

This website has no forms, accounts or advertising. We collect personal information only when you email us: your name, email address and whatever you choose to write.

Website statistics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they read. It uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location (city or region), device and browser type. It does not tell us who you are. We use these statistics only to improve our content and to report on our reach to funders. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

How we use it

We use it only to reply to you. We do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, uses Google Analytics, and links to our pages on social media platforms. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to khalid@canadalawarabic.ca. We handle personal information in line with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).
